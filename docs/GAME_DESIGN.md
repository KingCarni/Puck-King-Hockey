# Puck King Hockey - Game Design

## Elevator Pitch

Puck King Hockey is a chaotic arcade hockey roguelite RPG. Matches are short, violent, funny, and skill-based. Between matches, the player drafts upgrades, manages a weird roster, survives injuries, and pushes deeper through a branching run toward a championship boss.

## Design Pillars

### 1. Arcade First

The game should feel good in ten seconds. Skating, shooting, passing, and checking matter more than deep systems at the start.

### 2. Chaos With Control

Big hits, weird bounces, wild upgrades, and dramatic goals are encouraged, but the player should still feel responsible for wins and losses.

### 3. Players Become Stories

Roster members should become memorable through names, traits, injuries, gear, stats, and ridiculous moments.

### 4. Roguelite Decisions Matter

After each match, the player should make interesting choices: upgrade the team, heal someone, recruit, gamble, take a risky event, or prepare for a boss.

### 5. Stylized Over Realistic

This is not a hockey simulator. Readability, impact, personality, and comedy beat realism.

## Core Match Format

Initial target:

- 3v3 arcade hockey
- First to 3 goals
- One playable team
- One enemy team
- Simple goalie behavior
- Short match length: 3-5 minutes

Future options:

- 4v4 mode
- Boss arenas
- Hazard modifiers
- Tournament/rival matches

## Core Controls

Minimum prototype controls:

- Move / skate
- Hard skate / boost
- Shoot
- Pass
- Check
- Switch player

## Player Stats

Prototype stat list:

- Speed
- Shot
- Puck Control
- Checking
- Defense
- Stamina
- Grit
- Luck

## Player Classes

Prototype classes:

- Sniper: strong shot, weak checking
- Enforcer: heavy hits, slower skating
- Trickster: strong puck control, weaker defense
- Grinder: balanced, stamina-focused
- Veteran: aura/team buffs later
- Goalie: blocking and recovery

## Upgrade Examples

- Rocket Skates: increased speed, reduced balance
- Sticky Tape: improved puck control
- Titanium Pads: checks stun opponents
- Brass Knuckles: stronger checks, more penalty risk later
- Lucky Mouthguard: better clutch/luck rolls
- Haunted Puck: weird shot behavior

## Enemy Team Examples

- The Ice Rats: fast, fragile, annoying puck control
- The Parking Lot Kings: slow bruisers, huge hits
- The Corporate Sharks: referee/rules bias later
- The Bonebreakers: injury-heavy boss team

## MVP Feature

The MVP is not a full game. It is one fun hockey match plus one roguelite reward screen.

If skating, shooting, and checking are not fun, the RPG layer does not matter yet.
